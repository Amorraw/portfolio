Project · Foundations of Data Science

FoodHub delivery data — exploratory analysis

Situation: FoodHub, an NYC food-delivery aggregator, needed to understand demand patterns across 1,898 orders and 9 attributes (cuisine, cost, day of week, ratings, prep/delivery time).

Task: Answer specific business questions — which cuisines and days drive volume, how prep and delivery time distribute, and what ratings reveal about service quality.

Action: Full EDA in pandas/matplotlib/seaborn — univariate and bivariate analysis, cuisine-by-day demand breakdowns, and delivery/prep-time distribution analysis.

Result: Clear, data-backed answers on demand concentration, service-time bottlenecks, and rating drivers — the same analysis pattern used for any operational dataset.
Project · Classification & Hypothesis Testing

ExtraaLearn — lead-conversion classification

Situation: ExtraaLearn, an EdTech company, had 4,612 leads and needed to predict which would convert to paying customers, based on engagement signals like time on site and referral source.

Task: Build and compare classification models to identify high-probability leads and surface the strongest predictive features for the sales team.

Action: Trained and tuned Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and SVM (RBF kernel) via GridSearchCV; ranked feature importance using logistic-regression coefficients/odds.

Result: SVM (RBF) delivered the best recall among all models; time spent on website and first-interaction channel emerged as the top conversion drivers — directly actionable for a sales/marketing team.
Project · Recommendation Systems

Amazon Electronics — recommendation engine

Situation: A large-scale Amazon Electronics ratings dataset needed a recommendation approach that could suggest relevant products at scale.

Task: Build and evaluate multiple recommendation strategies — rank-based, collaborative filtering, and matrix factorization — and tune the strongest for accuracy.

Action: Implemented rank-based, user-user CF, item-item CF, and SVD (matrix factorization) models with the surprise library, tuning via GridSearchCV.

Result: Tuned user-user KNN cut RMSE from a 1.84 baseline to 1.11, with 0.858 precision and 0.644 F1 — the direct precursor to the recommendation logic used in the Cultura monorepo above.

Interview talking points

  • Comfortable across the full ML toolkit — EDA, classification, hypothesis testing, and collaborative-filtering recommenders — not just one narrow specialty.
  • Grounded in real evaluation discipline: baseline-vs-tuned metrics (RMSE, precision, recall, F1), not just "I trained a model."
  • Direct line from coursework to production: the recommendation-systems project fed straight into the Cultura monorepo's real recommendation logic.
